The filter area lets you narrow down the data.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the filter area">
    Open the filter area in the table header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose filters">
    Select the filters you want. Depending on the column, text search, dropdown
    lists or date ranges are available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    The table updates automatically and shows only matching processes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Resetting filters

Reset individual filters by removing the selection, or clear all filters via the
corresponding button.

<Showcase
  src="/videos/simptrack/filter-zuruecksetzen.mp4"
  caption="Remove individual filters or reset all filters."
/>

<Callout type="tip">
  Your filters are saved. The next time you open SimpTrack you'll immediately
  see your usual selection again.
</Callout>

## Export to Excel

The currently filtered view can be **exported to Excel** — exactly the rows and
columns you see are exported.
