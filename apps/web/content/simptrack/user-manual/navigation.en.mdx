## Scrolling

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scroll horizontally">
    Use the **mouse wheel** to scroll horizontally through the table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll vertically">
    Hold the **Shift key** and use the mouse wheel at the same time.
    Alternatively use the **scrollbars** at the edges.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sorting columns

1. Click a **column header**.
2. The table is sorted by that column.
3. Click again to **reverse** the sort order.

An **arrow icon** indicates the current sort.

## Reordering columns

1. Click and hold a **column header**.
2. Drag the column to the **desired position**.
3. Release the mouse button.

The new order is **saved automatically**.
