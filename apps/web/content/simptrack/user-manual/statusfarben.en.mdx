Table rows are highlighted by color so you can spot critical processes at a
glance.

| Color            | Meaning                                |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| **Red**          | Invoice is overdue                     |
| **Yellow**       | Invoice is active, but not yet overdue |
| **No highlight** | Process is completed                   |

<Callout type="warning">
  Rows highlighted in red usually require prompt action, because the invoice is
  already overdue.
</Callout>

The exact thresholds for "overdue" are defined during setup. Contact your
administrator if the classification differs from your expectations.
