title: SimpTrack
---

SimpTrack is a **dashboard widget** for monitoring and managing invoice and
workflow processes inside **JobRouter**. The table brings every running process
together in one place — filterable, sortable, and with direct access to
documents and process information.

## Features at a glance

<Tabs>
<Tab label="For users">

- **Filter and sort** invoices
- **Reorder** columns via drag-and-drop
- **Customize** the table view (column order, zoom)
- **Row actions**: open history and invoice directly
- **Open** documents and process information directly
- Set filters and **reset** them at any time
- **Filter presets** for quick access to frequently used views
- **Pagination** — adjust rows per page and navigate pages
- **Toolbar** with full screen, Excel export, dark mode and zoom
- **Settings** for filters, columns and templates
- **Export** data to Excel
- Personal settings are **saved automatically**

</Tab>
<Tab label="For integrators">

- Configured through a **single file** (`config.php`)
- Columns, filters and actions are fully **declarative**
- **Branding** via the `THEME` option
- Shipped as a **ready-made release zip** — no build step at the customer

</Tab>
</Tabs>
