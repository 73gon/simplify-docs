Presets save frequently used filter combinations and allow you to quickly apply
predefined views.

## Using a preset

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the preset selector">
    Open the preset selector in the toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a preset">Choose the preset you want to apply.</Step>
  <Step title="Filters are applied">
    The saved filters are applied automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Saving your own preset

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up the filters">
    Configure the filters you want to save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save filters">Click **"Save filters"**.</Step>
  <Step title="Enter a name">Enter a name for the preset.</Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Confirm the input. The preset is now available in the selector.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip">
  Presets can be managed, renamed and deleted in **Settings → Templates**.
</Callout>
